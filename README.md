### What I work on

- **Infrastructure & security:** pfSense firewall administration, WireGuard VPN, least-privilege access, NAS storage (RAID, backups), business continuity, incident response
- **Automation & AI:** Python, Google Apps Script, LLM integration (Claude API), Slack API, workflow automation, test automation (pytest, Playwright)
- **Data governance & compliance:** policy-as-code, file-naming compliance auditing, QA sampling and scoring, SOP development
- **IT operations:** budget ownership, procurement and vendor management, IT service desk and SLAs, asset lifecycle management, onboarding and offboarding, EOS
